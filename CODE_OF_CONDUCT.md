# Code of Conduct

Based on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org) v2.1, available
under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

## What we are going for

A place where someone in their first semester can ask a basic question without feeling stupid,
and where someone in their final year can be told they are wrong without taking it personally.

Everyone is welcome here regardless of age, disability, gender, experience level, education,
background, nationality, appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity.

## Please do

- Be patient with people who know less than you. You were new recently too.
- Give feedback on the work, with a reason, and a suggestion if you have one.
- Take feedback well, and own your mistakes.
- Assume people mean well. Most arguments here are misunderstandings.
- Credit other people's ideas.

## Please do not

- Harass, intimidate, or make discriminatory jokes.
- Attack a person instead of discussing their work.
- Share someone's private information.
- Use sexualised language or imagery, or give unwanted sexual attention.
- Be dismissive of beginners. Saying "this is obvious" or making fun of a question is not okay
  here, and it is the thing most likely to make someone leave and never come back.
- Derail discussions repeatedly.

## Where this applies

Everywhere the project happens: the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and reviews.
It also applies when you are representing the project at ACM IIT Mandi events or online.

## If something goes wrong

Tell a maintainer (see [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md)), or the ACM IIT Mandi core team.

Reports stay private. We will not share who reported something without asking first. A
maintainer involved in an incident steps back and does not handle it.

Depending on what happened, the response can be a private word, a warning, a temporary ban, or
a permanent one. This applies to maintainers exactly the same as to anyone else.
